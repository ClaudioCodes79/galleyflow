No calendar trip? Tap Try a sample flight on Home to walk through every step.

Common questions

My trip does not show up.

GalleyFlow reads calendar events titled "Trip: ...". Check that the event is in a calendar on this phone, that calendar access is on in Settings, and tap Refresh trips in the app's Settings.

I do not get timer notifications.

Allow notifications for GalleyFlow in the iPhone Settings app and keep Timers on in GalleyFlow's Settings.

The timer does not show on my lock screen.

Allow Live Activities when iOS asks, or turn them on for GalleyFlow in the iPhone Settings app.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Orders, trays, timers and the guide all work offline, including in flight. The flight tracking and turbulence links open web pages, which need a connection.

Can I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Ending a flight clears its service data, and deleting the app removes everything.
