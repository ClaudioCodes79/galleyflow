GalleyFlow

A pocket companion for the cabin service. It keeps drink orders, galley prep, trays and delivery in one place, and reminds you of what comes next.

What it does

  • Reads your trips from your calendar and fills in the flight for you.
  • Takes drink orders on a seat map that matches your aircraft.
  • Builds the pull list and loads trays in your own walking order.
  • Guides each delivery with a numbered route.
  • Timers for trash, lavatory checks, second service and wrap-up, on the lock screen too.

GalleyFlow is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any airline. It is guidance only: your airline's procedures and your purser always come first.
