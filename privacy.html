Privacy Policy

GalleyFlow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

What stays on your phone

Drink orders, seat notes, trays, timers, settings and flight details are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to any server, and there is no account to create.

Calendar access

If you allow it, GalleyFlow reads calendar events to find your trips (events titled "Trip: ...") and, if you use the Flighty app, the tracking link in its flight events. iOS calls this "Full Access" because it has no read-only option for calendars, but GalleyFlow only reads. It never creates, changes or deletes events, and calendar data never leaves your phone.

Notifications and Live Activities

Timer reminders and the lock screen timer are created on your phone. No data is sent to any server for them.

Links to other sites

Tapping a flight number or the Turbulence pill opens a public web page (a flight tracker or a turbulence forecast) in a browser view. Nothing from the app is sent with those requests, and GalleyFlow cannot see the pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Maps

The route map uses Apple Maps. Map tiles are loaded from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking and analytics

GalleyFlow contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Children

GalleyFlow is a tool for flight attendants and is not directed at children.
